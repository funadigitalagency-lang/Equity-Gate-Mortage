FAQ

Questions we get
every day

Do I pay anything to Equity Gate?

No. Our service is completely free to you. We receive a commission from the bank (typically 0.5–1% of the loan amount) once your loan completes. The bank absorbs this cost — not you.

Can I get an equity release loan if I live outside the UAE?

Yes. Non-resident property owners can access up to 65% LTV. We manage the entire process remotely — including document submission, bank liaison, and legal coordination.

What is the minimum property value needed?

Most banks require a minimum property value of AED 1,000,000 and a minimum loan of AED 250,000. We work across all price ranges up to AED 50M+.

My property already has a mortgage. Can I still release equity?

Yes — this is called a top-up loan. The bank will assess the current value of your property and allow you to borrow against the equity above your existing mortgage balance, up to the LTV cap.

What are the costs besides the loan itself?

Standard costs: 1% bank arrangement fee, 0.25% DLD mortgage registration fee, and AED 2,500–3,500 for property valuation. We present a full cost breakdown before you commit to anything.

What if I was rejected by my bank directly?

A rejection from one bank doesn't mean rejection from all. Different banks have different criteria for self-employed income, non-residents, mixed nationalities, and property types. We reanalyse your file and target the right lender.

How long does the whole process take?

For straightforward cases: 14–21 days from first call to fund disbursement. Complex cases or non-residents: 21–35 days. We have completed loans in as little as 10 working days.

Is my information kept confidential?

Absolutely. Your financial and personal information is shared only with banks you explicitly approve, and only for the purpose of your loan application. We never sell or share data with third parties.
